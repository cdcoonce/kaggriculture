## Where the hypothesis comes from, disclosed in full

The second-slot screen (`eval/prereg/2026-09-23-second-slot-screen.md`, band 896000, n=128)
selected and confirmed W30 on own bank. EH1 (`extra_hands: 1`) was not selected: its own-bank
lower bound was −$525 against a −$500 bar. But it recorded the **largest margin delta of any arm,
+$1,566 (se $541), for a one-sided lower bound of about +$676**. That was the only margin in the
screen confidently above zero. Margin was a reported diagnostic there, so this is a
**hypothesis generated by that data**, and nothing in that document is amended.

This document tests it on a fresh band, against a different baseline (S3W30, the confirmed best
configuration, not S3), and with a single arm, so no multiplicity correction applies. Selection
from the 896000 screen is the reason it is tested at all. That selection inflates the prior
point estimate, so the prediction below is shrunk toward zero.

## Why margin is the primary quantity here

`eval/prereg/2026-09-07-elo-aligned-factorial.md`'s post-ladder addendum states what decides a live
game: whether our bank exceeds the opponent's. The per-game margin (own − opponent) is exactly
that quantity, and its paired delta is what a configuration changes about it. Own bank was used
before because it was the only quantity with an interval. `tools/recon-scripts/second_slot_verdict.py`
now gives margin an interval computed from the ledger rows. That script reproduced #170's −$585 to
the dollar, and its own-bank means match the ledger's `mean_delta` exactly, so its pairing is verified.

Own bank still acts as a guard. An arm that wins margin by shrinking our own bank is not
acceptable, because the +$10k ≈ +100 Elo slope says own bank transfers against the field.

The substitution risk is stated in advance. W30 and EH1 are both labor levers: W30 frees crew
from wheat throughput, and EH1 adds a hand. This project has recorded two knobs acting as
substitutes before (HML20 × `valve_soft_threshold`, interaction −$518). EH1's effect on top of W30
may be smaller than its effect on top of S3.

## Decision rule (fixed before launch)

Pooling is the same as in the second-slot screen: per-leader mean and se, a simple mean across
the three leaders, and pooled se `sqrt(sum se²)/3`. Margin is computed with
`tools/recon-scripts/second_slot_verdict.py`'s paired per-seed method.

**INVALID** if any of these hold:

- the engine is not 1.32.7;
- the pre-flight fails;
- any crash-type veto, or a `baseline_degenerate` or `opponent_degenerate` veto.

**CONFIRMED** only if all hold:

1. pooled **margin** delta one-sided 95% lower bound **> $0**;
2. pooled **own-bank** delta point estimate **≥ $0**, and its one-sided 95% lower bound
   **> −$500**;
3. no single leader's own-bank point estimate below **−$2,000**.

**NOT CONFIRMED** otherwise. No re-run, band reuse, or threshold change after launch.

REPORTED, NOT GATING: per-leader own-bank and margin deltas, pooled MDE for both, `coupled_draws`,
and win counts against each leader.

### Script extension — `tools/recon-scripts/second_slot_verdict.py`

The second-slot precedent's script computed the margin's one-sided 95% **upper** bound only
(that prereg's CONFIRMED veto reads a margin confidently *worse* than $0). This prereg's primary
criterion reads the margin's one-sided 95% **lower** bound instead, and its REPORTED block also
wants margin's `mde_80`, neither of which existed in the script. Both were added
(`pooled_margin_lb95`, `pooled_margin_mde_80`), computed from the same `pooled_margin`/
`pooled_margin_se` the UB already used, with no change to any existing computation or print line
(only new lines appended).

**Byte-identical reproduction on the old inputs, confirmed**: re-running the extended script
against the confirm-band W30 ledgers (897000) and the screen-band EH1 ledgers (896000) reproduces
every existing figure exactly — pooled own Δ $519/$763, own-bank LB $140/−$525, `mde_80`
$574/$1,958, margin UB $495/$2,457, and all three per-leader pairing checks (`own_bank_check_ok`)
pass for both groups. The new margin LB for the 896000 EH1 screen data comes out to **+$676**,
which matches this document's own disclosed figure ("a one-sided lower bound of about +$676") to
the dollar — an independent check on the new arithmetic, not just the old.
